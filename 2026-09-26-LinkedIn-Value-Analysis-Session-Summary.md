# LinkedIn value analysis, session summary

## The decision

As LinkedIn is currently run, there is no traceable value in the record. Stop using the company page as a broadcast channel. Keep LinkedIn only as one weekly Hub link each from Bridgette and Dianne on their personal profiles, UTM tagged, with no workshop ask. Run that for 90 days to the December half-year report, then kill it if fewer than five UTM-tracked registrations or Pipedrive deals trace back to LinkedIn.

The fair verdict is "unproven and neglected", not "tested and failed". The channel was never operated the way the plan said it should be, and the attribution plumbing that would have proven it either way was not in place.

## Why nothing showed up

1. **The reach multiplier was never switched on.** The plan relied on Bridgette and Dianne posting weekly from personal profiles, which the strategy estimated at five to ten times company page reach. The June status summary marked this "blocked". No evidence was found that it started. The company page alone, at 188 followers, cannot fill a workshop room.

2. **Attribution was blind.** Two GA4 measurement IDs, no LinkedIn column in the registration register, and campaign emails sending without UTMs. A good LinkedIn month would have been logged as "website" or "direct".

3. **Content inverted the 3:1 rule.** Recent posts skewed to "Register now" asks and drew zero comments. The Risk and Audit pack had ten posts and two carousels ready but the trust-first cadence did not land.

4. **The Lyndal Lane AI posting pilot did not visibly go live.** Agreed 20 July, it needed admin access to the IPS LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram and Google Business accounts. No trace of posts or access being granted was found in email or transcripts. Later meetings with Lyndal were about her SaaS product and the AI Pod, not IPS execution.

5. **The strategy has moved on.** The 30 August web brief and the 2 September visibility program are built on the TNA form (580 leads in six months) and AI findability (cited 40 per cent, named 16 per cent, ChatGPT 0). LinkedIn is not mentioned in the 33-page visibility program. The 2 September exec meeting listed it only as "ongoing engagement". Caoimhe's task list already runs into 2027, and she becomes sole marketing owner on 1 October.

## Recommendation in full

- **Company page:** stop broadcast posting now. Do not delete it, since a dormant page is a trust leak for anyone cross-checking. Limit it to resharing clinician posts 24 hours later.
- **Personal profiles:** Bridgette and Dianne each post one Knowledge Hub link a week, UTM tagged, with Andrea's edit and no workshop ask. Named clinicians on LinkedIn double as the earned mentions and E-E-A-T signals the ghost-citation experiment needs, so this is not a separate marketing job.
- **Precondition:** Fix 3 in the Phase 0 attribution note (Google Analytics and e-commerce tracking on MailerLite campaign sends) must be live first, or the test is unmeasurable again.
- **Window:** 90 days, ending with the December half-year report.
- **Kill criteria:** fewer than five UTM-tracked registrations or Pipedrive deals sourced to LinkedIn, or the clinicians not sustaining weekly cadence by end of October.

## Assumptions and doubts

- Assumption: the June audit figures were current as of mid June and nothing changed materially before the export was taken. The export will confirm or overturn this.
- The Shopify source data is a 60-day window and records last click, so email and social are systematically under-counted. LinkedIn at zero may be partly an artefact of that, but it is also consistent with every other signal.
- Personal profile reach is inferred as unrealised from the "blocked" status and absence of evidence, not from profile analytics, which only Bridgette and Dianne can export.
